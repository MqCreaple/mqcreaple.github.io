Example: Pendulum on a Cart

States: $ \mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x & \dot{x} & \theta & \dot{\theta} \end{bmatrix}^T $

$$ T = \frac12 M \dot{x}^2 + \frac12 m\left[\left(\dot{x} + l\cos\theta\,\dot{\theta}\right)^2 + \left(l\sin\theta\,\dot{\theta}\right)^2\right] $$

$$ V = mgl(1 - \cos\theta) $$

Lagrangian:

$$ \mathcal{L} = T - V $$

Define:

$$D = M + m\sin^2\theta, Q = ml\dot{\theta}^2\sin\theta, M_\text{tot} = M + m$$

Then the system equations derived from Lagrangian is:

$$ \ddot{x} = \frac{F_\text{ext} + Q + mg\sin\theta\cos\theta - \tau_\mathrm{ext}\cos\theta/l}{D} $$

$$ \ddot{\theta} = \frac{M_\text{tot}(\tau_\mathrm{ext} - mgl\sin\theta) - ml(F_\text{ext}+Q)\cos\theta}{ml^2D} $$

Example: Pendulum on a Cart

Linearising the system around the topmost position $(x=0, \theta=\pi)$ gives the result:

$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\begin{bmatrix}x \\ \dot{x} \\ \theta-\pi \\ \dot{\theta}\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}0 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & \frac{mg}{M} & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & \frac{M_\text{tot}g}{Ml} & 0\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}x \\ \dot{x} \\ \theta-\pi \\ \dot{\theta}\end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix}0 & 0 \\ \frac{1}{M} & \frac{1}{Ml} \\ 0 & 0 \\ \frac{1}{Ml} & \frac{M_\text{tot}}{Ml^2m}\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}F_\text{ext} \\ \tau_\mathrm{ext}\end{bmatrix} $$
